Research &

I combine electromagnetic simulation and computational optimization with device layout, fabrication, and testing. My research includes on-chip metasurfaces, optical computing, reinforcement learning for photonic design, and wafer-level optics for cold-atom applications.

More recently, I developed inverse-design methods and system–device optimization workflows for diffractive AR waveguides.

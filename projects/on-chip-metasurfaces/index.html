Project 03 · Integrated photonics

Shaping light and computing on a chip

On-chip metasurfaces for wavefront control, optical signal processing, and image classification.

Silicon photonicsOptical computingFabrication & test

The research question

Can metasurface concepts be translated to guided waves on a chip, enabling compact wavefront control and optical computation?

My contribution

  • Developed the design principle and performed numerical simulations for on-chip wavefront shaping.
  • Co-fabricated devices, performed optical measurements, and analyzed the results with collaborators.
  • Conceived and co-developed an integrated photonic metasystem for image classification at telecommunication wavelengths.
  • Built Python/PyTorch models and translated optical designs into parameterized GDSpy layouts.

Wavefront control on silicon

The 2019 work adapted dielectric metasurfaces to a silicon-on-insulator platform, enabling guided-wave wavefront shaping and optical signal processing.

Optical image classification

The 2022 work used cascaded metasurfaces to perform wavelength-selective classification on a silicon chip. The fabricated system demonstrated spatial-pattern recognition under both continuous-wave and femtosecond-pulse illumination.

Experiment · continuous wave
92%
Spatial-pattern classification accuracy with narrow-band illumination.
Experiment · femtosecond pulses
89%
Spatial-pattern classification accuracy with broadband pulse illumination.

These results apply to the spatial-pattern classifier reported in the paper; they are not general-purpose image-recognition benchmarks.

Design, layout, and fabrication

My doctoral work connected computational design to cleanroom fabrication, optical characterization, and layouts prepared for AIM Photonics tapeout.

Project 02 · Optical simulation

Connecting AR systems to grating design

A computational workflow linking system propagation, spatial gratings, and device optimization.

System modelingJAX / FMMaxDesign automation

The design problem

A diffractive augmented-reality (AR) waveguide connects a system-level optical target to the behavior of individual gratings. Device geometry, propagation paths, and light distribution at the eyebox must be considered together.

My contribution

  • Developed inverse-design and optical system–device co-optimization methods for diffractive AR waveguides.
  • Built a user interface connecting design parameters and imported CAD geometry to system and device analysis.
  • Used lookup-table search and FMMax refinement for spatial grating design.

A connected simulation workflow

The interface brings parameter inputs, geometry inspection, propagation analysis, and grating optimization into one workflow. The examples below show the system geometry and optical cross sections used in the design process.

Engineering focus

This work demonstrates optical modeling, numerical optimization, and the development of tools that connect system specifications to device geometry.

The images show simulation and software results. This case study does not claim measured headset performance or a production deployment.
